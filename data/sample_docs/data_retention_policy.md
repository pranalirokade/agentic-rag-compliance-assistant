# Data Retention Policy (Fictional - Acme Industrial Systems)

## Purpose and scope
This policy defines how long Acme Industrial Systems keeps business records and how they are disposed of. It applies to all employees, contractors and systems that store company or customer data.

## Retention periods
Financial records are retained for 8 years from the end of the financial year to which they relate.
Employee records are retained for 6 years after the employee's termination date.
Customer support tickets are retained for 3 years after the ticket is closed.
System access logs are retained for 12 months and then deleted automatically.
Marketing consent records are retained for 5 years after consent is withdrawn or expires.

## Secure disposal
Data that has passed its retention period must be securely deleted within 30 days. Paper records are cross-cut shredded. Electronic media are sanitised using a certified wipe that follows NIST SP 800-88 guidance.

## Legal holds
A legal hold suspends all deletion for the affected records. Only the Legal department can place or lift a legal hold, and Legal must record the reason and the date in the hold register.
