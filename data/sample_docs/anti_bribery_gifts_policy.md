# Anti-Bribery and Gifts Policy (Fictional - Acme Industrial Systems)

## Gifts and hospitality
Any gift or hospitality with a value above USD 50 must be declared in the gift register within 5 working days. Cash and cash equivalents such as gift cards may never be offered or accepted.

## Public officials
Hospitality for public officials requires written pre-approval from the Compliance team. Facilitation payments are prohibited in all circumstances, even where they are customary locally.

## Training and speaking up
All employees complete anti-bribery training once a year. Concerns can be raised through the confidential speak-up hotline, and retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is prohibited.
