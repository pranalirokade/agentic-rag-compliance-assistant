# Remote Work and Device Policy (Fictional - Acme Industrial Systems)

## Company devices
Work must be done on company-managed laptops with full-disk encryption enabled. Personal USB storage devices are not permitted. Software may only be installed from the approved software catalogue.

## Networks
When working from a public Wi-Fi network, employees must connect through the company VPN before accessing any internal system.

## Screen locking
Devices must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.

## Lost or stolen devices
A lost or stolen device must be reported to the IT service desk within 2 hours of discovering the loss. IT will remotely lock and wipe the device, and the employee must also file a security incident report.
