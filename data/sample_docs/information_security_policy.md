# Information Security Policy (Fictional - Acme Industrial Systems)

## Access control
Access is granted on the principle of least privilege. Managers review the access rights of their team every quarter and revoke anything that is no longer needed.

## Passwords and authentication
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all remote access and for every administrator account. Passwords must never be shared or stored in plain text.

## Encryption
Confidential data must be encrypted at rest using AES-256. Data in transit must use TLS 1.2 or higher. Encryption keys are rotated at least once a year.

## Patch management
Critical vulnerabilities must be patched within 7 days of the patch being released. High severity vulnerabilities must be patched within 30 days. Exceptions require written approval from the Chief Information Security Officer.
