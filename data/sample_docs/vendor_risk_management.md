# Vendor Risk Management Standard (Fictional - Acme Industrial Systems)

## Vendor tiering
Tier 1 vendors are those that process or can access confidential data or that support critical operations. Tier 2 vendors have limited access to internal systems. Tier 3 vendors have no access to data or systems.

## Due diligence
Tier 1 vendors must complete the security questionnaire before onboarding and must provide a current SOC 2 Type II report. Tier 1 vendors are reassessed every year. Tier 2 vendors are reassessed every two years.

## Contract requirements
Contracts with Tier 1 vendors must include a right to audit, breach notification obligations, and data return or deletion clauses at the end of the contract.

## Offboarding
When a vendor relationship ends, all vendor access to Acme systems must be revoked within 24 hours and the vendor must certify deletion of Acme data.
