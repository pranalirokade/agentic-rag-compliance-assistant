# Security Incident Response SOP (Fictional - Acme Industrial Systems)

## Severity levels
SEV1 is a confirmed breach or outage affecting customers or confidential data. SEV2 is a contained incident with limited impact. SEV3 is a minor event or near miss.

## Reporting
Any employee who suspects a security incident must report it to the security team within 1 hour of noticing it, using the security hotline or the incident form on the intranet. Do not try to investigate on your own or delete evidence.

## Triage and response
The on-call responder begins triage of a SEV1 incident within 15 minutes of the report. The incident commander decides on containment actions and keeps a written timeline of every decision.

## Notification
If personal data is affected, affected customers and the regulator must be notified within 72 hours of Acme becoming aware of the breach. Legal approves all external notifications.

## Post-incident review
A blameless post-incident review is held within 5 business days of closing a SEV1 or SEV2 incident, and corrective actions are tracked to completion.
